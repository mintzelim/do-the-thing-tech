## What not to do

Do not buy traffic, solicit ad clicks, place ads where visitors may confuse them with navigation, add fake reviews/ratings, create thin near-duplicate posts, or request a review repeatedly without material improvements. Google prohibits artificial clicks and misleading site or ad behavior.[5]

Do not treat the number of posts, word count, traffic volume, GA4 tags, or `ads.txt` alone as an approval threshold. Google does not publish a universal article-count or traffic minimum in its eligibility guidance.[1] The practical question is whether a reviewer can quickly see original publisher value, accurate identity, clear navigation, responsible health-adjacent information, and a non-deceptive reader experience.

## Recommended sequence

1. Allow the scheduled 24-hour check to confirm the first production data and refreshed Google status.
2. If Low value content remains, obtain approval for the second editorial batch and complete the claim-to-source review.
3. Run the final crawl, policy, authorship/disclosure, and consent checks.
4. Present a short evidence-based readiness checklist to the user.
5. Request an AdSense review **only after the user gives fresh, explicit approval**.
