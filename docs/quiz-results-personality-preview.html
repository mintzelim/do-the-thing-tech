QUIZ RESULTS · PERSONALITY-STYLE PASS

Seven results that make sense even if you have no ADHD vocabulary.

This pass keeps the seven existing scoring keys, friendly pattern names, official-framework mapping, and non-diagnostic boundary. It changes only the reader-facing explanation: start with a recognisable everyday moment, make the upside concrete, and offer one tiny kind move. Each result also gets its own consistent purple-mascot scene.

Plain firstNo clinical shorthand is needed to understand the result. Any framework language stays in the existing “Worth knowing” note.

Specific, not vagueEvery pattern starts with a situation a reader can recognise in daily life, then explains why it can be tricky.

Warm, not diagnosingThese are self-reflection patterns, not a medical label or a claim about what anyone must be.

READY, SET, GO!

YOUR PATTERN

The Spark Starter

You spot a thing that needs doing and your brain presses the big green GO button. Starting is easy; the boring middle and the last ten percent may feel like a completely different sport.

  • THE TRICKY BITYou can be halfway into a new plan before your future self has agreed to finish it.
  • YOUR BRIGHT SIDEWhen everyone is stuck at the starting line, you are often the person who gets the room moving.
  • A KIND NEXT STEPBefore you start, write: “I’m done when…” Give yourself a finish line while the spark is still hot.
A REMINDER FOR YOUYour enthusiasm is not a problem. It just deserves a tiny map.
PING… WHERE’D IT GO?

YOUR PATTERN

The Thought Wanderer

Your brain has plenty to say, but a task can quietly wander off-screen when it is boring, invisible, or not happening right now. That does not mean you do not care.

  • THE TRICKY BITImportant things can vanish from view until they suddenly become very, very urgent.
  • YOUR BRIGHT SIDEWhen something catches your interest, you can notice connections and ideas other people miss.
  • A KIND NEXT STEPPut one cue directly in your path: the item itself, a note on the door, or one calendar alert.
A REMINDER FOR YOUOut of sight is not out of care. Make what matters easier to bump into.
DEEP IN THE ZONE

YOUR PATTERN

The Deep-Dive Mind

Once your brain grabs an idea, it can hold on with both hands. That focus can be brilliant; changing channels can feel like being asked to leave a good book on the last page.

  • THE TRICKY BITSwitching tasks, ending a thought loop, or letting a small issue go can take more effort than people see.
  • YOUR BRIGHT SIDEYou can stay with a meaningful idea long enough to find the good bits other people rush past.
  • A KIND NEXT STEPChoose one hand-off cue—a timer, a song, or a stretch—then give yourself one minute to change gears.
A REMINDER FOR YOUYou can pause without losing the thing you care about.
SIGNAL INCOMING

YOUR PATTERN

The Signal Catcher

You may notice timing, tone, memory clues, and feelings very quickly. Useful? Absolutely. A bit like having the volume set higher than everyone else? Also possible.

  • THE TRICKY BITA busy moment, interruption, or emotional wobble can land bigger than you expected.
  • YOUR BRIGHT SIDEYou can pick up nuance and emotional detail that helps you understand a situation deeply.
  • A KIND NEXT STEPWhen a moment feels loud, take three slow breaths and name the next action in six words or fewer.
A REMINDER FOR YOUA loud moment can have a small, steady answer.
LOW-BATTERY MODE

YOUR PATTERN

The Mood-Powered Mind

Some days, a normal task looks normal. On other days, replying to one email looks like climbing a mountain in flip-flops. Your energy and mood can change the size of the job.

  • THE TRICKY BITWhen you are low on fuel, even a small task can arrive wearing a dramatic cape.
  • YOUR BRIGHT SIDEYou bring real care and meaning to the people and projects that matter to you.
  • A KIND NEXT STEPLower the bar on purpose: try the five-minute version and count showing up as a real win.
A REMINDER FOR YOUSmall effort is still effort. Today still counts.
TOO MANY TABS

YOUR PATTERN

The Many-Tabs Mind

Your brain can feel like sixteen tabs are playing at once: ideas, noise, energy, reminders, and one mystery thought from 2014. It is a lot of input, not a personal failure.

  • THE TRICKY BITEverything can ask for your attention at the same time, which makes choosing one thing feel weirdly hard.
  • YOUR BRIGHT SIDEYou can generate energy, possibilities, and fast connections at a remarkable pace.
  • A KIND NEXT STEPMake one quieter pocket first: mute one alert, lower one sound, or step away for a minute.
A REMINDER FOR YOUYou do not have to answer every signal at once.
SCOUTING AHEAD

YOUR PATTERN

The Careful Scout

Your brain is excellent at spotting what could go wrong. That can make you thoughtful and prepared—and it can also send you on a midnight fact-finding mission when a simple answer would do.

  • THE TRICKY BITA useful “what if?” can grow into a full-screen warning before you notice it has taken over.
  • YOUR BRIGHT SIDEYou think ahead, notice risks, and care about doing right by people.
  • A KIND NEXT STEPPut both feet on the floor, breathe out a little longer than you breathe in, then choose the kindest two-minute action.
A REMINDER FOR YOUYou can be careful without carrying every possible outcome.
