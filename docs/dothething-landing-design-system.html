Playful image-free modules

Keep the existing illustrated-card system when a transparent mascot or image genuinely explains the content. For text-first information, bring in a little more DoTheThing personality through an object-like module, a small quest cue, or a progress marker—never an empty image placeholder.

Selection rule: choose one playful module system for an entire content group. The fun comes from a clear metaphor and tiny pixel details; the reading stays calm, structured, and unmistakably Inter-led.

INTERACTION SYSTEM

Six-card group interactions

These directions animate a group of six related cards as one system, rather than treating every card as an isolated button. Hover or focus the collection to see the relationship between cards; individual card content remains readable before any movement begins.

Use rule: reserve a group interaction for six related cards with a shared topic. Keep the animation under 200ms, use it to reinforce relationship rather than hide content, and preserve the same clear grid when reduced motion is enabled.
