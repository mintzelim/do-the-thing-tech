# Targeted SEO Improvement Sources

## Core clinical and management context

The National Institute of Mental Health describes ADHD as a developmental disorder involving ongoing inattention, hyperactivity, and/or impulsivity that can affect functioning at school, work, home, and in relationships. It notes that standard treatment may include medication and psychosocial interventions. [NIMH: ADHD](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/attention-deficit-hyperactivity-disorder-adhd)

The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention notes that treatment plans can involve medication and behavioral approaches and that diagnosis and treatment choices should be discussed with a healthcare provider. [CDC: ADHD](https://www.cdc.gov/adhd/about/index.html)

## Workplace accommodations

The current Job Accommodation Network ADHD page is available at [askjan.org/disabilities/Attention-Deficit-Hyperactivity-Disorder-AD-HD.cfm](https://askjan.org/disabilities/Attention-Deficit-Hyperactivity-Disorder-AD-HD.cfm). Search results identify examples such as a quieter workspace, noise-control tools, and remote work when appropriate. The legacy `...ADHD.cfm` URL used in the workplace article returned a 404 and should be replaced.

## Sleep

The National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute explains that sleep deficiency can impair focus, learning, reactions, and social functioning, and it distinguishes insufficient sleep from poor-quality sleep and sleep disorders. [NHLBI: Sleep Deprivation and Deficiency](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/sleep-deprivation)

## Writing boundaries

Updated articles should present practical, non-diagnostic suggestions; retain medical disclaimers; avoid presenting medication, accommodations, therapy, or sleep treatment as individualized advice; and link readers to appropriate professionals when symptoms, safety, or medication decisions are involved.
