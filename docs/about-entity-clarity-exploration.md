# About Page Entity Clarity — Approval-Only Comparison

> **Status: approval only.** This document does not change the live About page.

## What the live page already establishes

The current About page clearly names the founder, lived-experience context, audience, ADHD and executive-function framing, six product capabilities, workflow, ownership, support route, and the tool’s medical boundary. The scanner’s residual non-schema flag is most plausibly caused by the hero subtitle, which currently frames the page as an explanation of why the app exists rather than stating the business category, audience, and differentiator in one direct sentence at the top of the page.

## Proposed hero-only clarification

| Surface | Current copy | Approval-only proposed copy |
|---|---|---|
| Hero subtitle under **ABOUT DO THE THING** | Understanding ADHD, Task Management, and Why We Built This App | **DoTheThing is a free online task-breakdown tool for people with ADHD and executive-function friction. Turn brain dumps into smaller steps, focus-aware estimates, and an editable plan you can start today.** |

## Why this is constrained

The proposed replacement affects only the open hero. It makes all three required points explicit in one factual statement: **what the business is** (a free online task-breakdown tool), **who it serves** (people with ADHD and executive-function friction), and **how it differs** (brain dumps, focus-aware estimates, and editable steps). It preserves all content after the hero fold, including the creator story, mission, audience, practical capabilities, workflow, footer, and existing schema.

## Decision

| Direction | Result |
|---|---|
| **A — Keep current hero** | Make no website change; ask the audit tool to refresh its cached analysis. |
| **B — Apply hero clarification** | Replace only the current subtitle with the exact proposed sentence, then validate source, SSR metadata, desktop, and mobile rendering. |
